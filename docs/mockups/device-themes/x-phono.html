THRASHER THEME

Phonograph

A Victorian wax cylinder recorder in mahogany and brass. You speak into the horn and your clips sit in labelled cylinder tins.
  • The record button is the brass horn bell with a red wax seal
  • The level meter is a flyball governor that spreads as you get louder
  • Timer is a brass odometer and a wax cylinder spins on the mandrel
  • Import ask and widget become ivory keys under the horn
  • Project picker is a brass card holder and clips are cylinder tins
  • Footer is the reproducer rail with a stylus carriage
PORTRAITImport ask and widget moved out of the toolbar into a key row under the horn. Tin lids play a clip.
WIDEThe whole left side is the machine: cylinder and reproducer on top, governor and horn below, keys at the foot. Crank key imports, ear trumpet asks, pocket watch opens the widget, the brass gear is settings.
