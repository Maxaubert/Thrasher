THRASHER THEME

Pinball

The whole app is a pinball cabinet. Backglass up top, playfield below, and every control is a part of the machine.
  • Timer status and mic name glow on an orange dot matrix
  • REC is the launch plunger in the shooter lane
  • Import ask and widget are the three pop bumpers
  • Input level is a row of rollover lane lights
  • Clips are lit arrow inserts you hit to play
  • Left flipper button runs the player, the ball rides the progress rail
New fonts Monoton (backglass art only) and Barlow Condensed, both OFL. The dot matrix is drawn, no font.
