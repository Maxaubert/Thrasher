THRASHER THEME
Pocket Synth

The app becomes a tiny white aluminium sampler. A mono screen, a few knobs and sixteen numbered keys. Each clip is a pad.

  • Recorder is a pixel screen with a segment meter
  • Import ask and widget become keys beside REC
  • Clips are numbered keys, the selected one glows orange
  • Wide view lays clips out as a 4 by 2 pad bank
