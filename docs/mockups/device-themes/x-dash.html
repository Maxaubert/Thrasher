THRASHER THEME

Car Dashboard

A calm electric car cockpit. The level meter is the speed arc, the steering wheel holds the controls, and your clips are the playlist on the centre touchscreen.

  • Recorder becomes the driver display with a speed style arc meter
  • Record is the steering wheel hub, the toolbar icons and settings sit on the spoke buttons
  • Clips are a media playlist on a glass centre screen
  • Footer is the now playing media card

Portrait. Arc gauge left, wheel right. Hub records. Left spoke import and ask, right spoke widget and settings (the gear moved out of the title bar). Source chip is the project picker.

Wide. Driver display on the left: big arc with the timer as the speed readout, the wheel below with record on the hub and four spoke buttons. The centre screen on the right holds the source chip Inbox, search and the playlist. The media card plays across the bottom.
